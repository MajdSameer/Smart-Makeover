# Smart Makeover

## Register

Brand. A single-page marketing site where design IS the product: the visitor's impression of craft is the proof of the company's craft. Every surface serves one goal: turn Sydney homeowners into quote requests and phone calls.

## What this is

Conversion-focused, single-file marketing website for Smart Makeover, Sydney's kitchen, bathroom and laundry MAKEOVER specialists (Unit 4/100 Long St, Smithfield NSW). Hosted anywhere as one self-contained `index.html`.

CORE POSITIONING (from the real business, smartmakeover.com.au): a makeover is the alternative to a renovation, not a kind of one. If the cabinetry carcasses or bathroom structure are sound, Smart Makeover resurfaces and modernises the room: a third of the renovation cost, done in about 10 days, no demolition, no mess, less landfill. The site's job is to sell makeover-vs-renovation, never to read like a renovation builder.

They are price-anchored against full renovations, anxious about mess and weeks without a kitchen, and decide on proof: before/after photos, the 1/3-cost and 10-day claims, Google reviews, licence and insurance. Built, warm-grained, certain. Like oiled hardwood and a laser level line: warm material, exact execution. Premium without being precious; a builder you'd trust with your keys.

## Visual language

- Palette strategy: Committed, derived from the real Smart Makeover logo (red, navy and light blue chevrons). Deep navy carries the dark bands (hero, vs panel, final CTA, footer); pure white and a light-blue tint between; the logo red is the single CTA accent, under 10% of surface. The chevron mark is redrawn as an inline SVG in the header, footer and favicon.
- Type: Schibsted Grotesk (crisp, premium grotesque) for display, Hanken Grotesk (clean humanist) for body. Contrast on the display/text axis; tabular figures for numbers.
- Motion: restrained and purposeful. Strong ease-out curves, sub-300ms UI transitions, a cinematic full-bleed hero load-in (photo settles from a slight zoom while copy rises), a pure-CSS sticky-deck process sequence, a one-time slider sweep that teaches the before/after affordance, scroll reveals that enhance already-visible content, full prefers-reduced-motion fallback.
- Imagery: full-bleed hero photography, photographic bento tiles, one interactive before/after comparison slider. All stock placeholders are labelled swap slots.

## Anti-references (what this must NOT look like)

- The AI-default tradie template: cream/beige body, brass accents, three equal service cards, eyebrow label over every section.
- Generic franchise renovation sites: stock-smiling-couple heroes, "We Do Renovations!" headlines, badge clutter.
- Trend-chasing agency sites: scroll-hijacks and gimmicks that get between an anxious homeowner and the phone number.
