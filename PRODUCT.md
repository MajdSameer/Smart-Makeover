# Smart Makeover

## Register

Brand. A single-page marketing site where design IS the product: the visitor's impression of craft is the proof of the company's craft. Every surface serves one goal: turn Sydney homeowners into quote requests and phone calls.

## What this is

Conversion-focused, single-file marketing website for Smart Makeover, a Sydney residential renovation builder (kitchens, bathrooms, laundries and extras, full home makeovers). Hosted anywhere as one self-contained `index.html`.

## Target users

Sydney homeowners planning a high-ticket renovation ($20k-$300k). They are comparing builders, are anxious about cost blowouts, timelines and tradie reliability, and decide on trust signals: licence, insurance, guarantees, real photos, local reviews. They visit on mobile first, often in the evening.

## Conversion goals (priority order)

1. Phone call: tel link to 0401 939 291, visible at every breakpoint.
2. "Get a Free Quote" form submission (no backend yet; Formspree slot marked).
3. Primary CTA never out of reach: sticky header CTA on desktop, sticky call/quote bar on mobile.

## Brand personality

Built, warm-grained, certain. Like oiled hardwood and a laser level line: warm material, exact execution. Premium without being precious; a builder you'd trust with your keys.

## Visual language

- Palette strategy: Committed. Deep forest ink carries the dark bands (hero, guarantee, final CTA, footer); pure white between; honey (oklch 0.764 0.120 77.1) as the single accent, under 10% of surface.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque for display, Hanken Grotesk for body and UI. Tabular figures for numbers.
- Motion: restrained and purposeful. Strong ease-out curves, sub-300ms UI transitions, one orchestrated hero load-in, scroll reveals that enhance already-visible content, full prefers-reduced-motion fallback.
- Imagery: real renovation photography (verified stock as swappable placeholders), one interactive before/after comparison slider.

## Anti-references (what this must NOT look like)

- The AI-default tradie template: cream/beige body, brass accents, three equal service cards, eyebrow label over every section.
- Generic franchise renovation sites: stock-smiling-couple heroes, "We Do Renovations!" headlines, badge clutter.
- Trend-chasing agency sites: scroll-hijacks and gimmicks that get between an anxious homeowner and the phone number.

## Hard content rules

- No invented reviews, licence numbers, or statistics. Placeholders are explicit: [X], [ABN], [LICENCE NO], [REVIEW QUOTE], [EMAIL].
- Real phone number throughout: 0401 939 291.
- Australian English spelling (colour, organise, licence).
